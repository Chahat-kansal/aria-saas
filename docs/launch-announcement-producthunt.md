Hi PH! 👋 We're launching Aria — the first POS built around autonomous AI agents for Australian retail.

**The problem:** Every POS system shows you dashboards. None of them do anything. You still manually reorder stock, manually adjust prices, manually schedule staff, manually analyse reports.

**What Aria does differently:**

- **Reorder Agent** — checks stock levels every morning, drafts purchase orders, emails suppliers when you approve (one tap)
- **Pricing Agent** — scrapes competitor prices, flags margin opportunities, schedules price changes
- **Smart Schedule** — generates staff rosters from your sales demand forecast, emails staff when published
- **Conversation Reports** — type "why is wine slow this week?" and get an answer with a chart in 10 seconds

**Built for Australia:**
- A$ pricing throughout
- RSA compliance tracking
- GST handling
- AU data residency (Sydney)

**Pricing:** Starter $59/outlet/mo · Growth $129 · Autonomous $249 · 14-day free trial
